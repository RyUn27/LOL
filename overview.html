tripleS Overview

tripleS (Hangul: 트리플에스, Japanese: トリプルS), also known as SSS and SocialSonyoSeoul, is a multinational 24-member South Korean girl group under MODHAUS. The group officially debuted on February 13, 2023 with the mini album ASSEMBLE and its title track “Rising”, featuring the first ten revealed members. They later returned as a complete 24-member group on May 8, 2024 with their first studio album ASSEMBLE24 and the title track “Girls Never Die.”

tripleS operates through a unique fan-participation system in which fans vote to form rotational sub-units and influence major group activities. The first sub-unit, Acid Angel from Asia, debuted on October 28, 2022, followed by +(KR)ystal Eyes (May 4, 2023), LOVElution (August 17, 2023), EVOLution (October 11, 2023), and Aria (January 15, 2024).

The group currently has thirteen official sub-units: Acid Angel from Asia, +(KR)ystal Eyes, LOVElution, EVOLution, NXT, Aria, Glow, Visionary Vision, ∞!, Moon, Sun, Neptune and Zenith, as well as the collaborative unit ACID EYES. NXT and Glow were created to introduce the final members before the full 24-member lineup was completed.

Name Meaning

The name tripleS stands for SocialSonyoSeoul, representing “social girls from Seoul.” It also symbolizes small “s” trainees coming together to form one big “S,” meaning the complete group.

History

2022 – Formation & First Unit

MODHAUS announced tripleS as a new type of K-pop group built on fan participation. From May onward, the first ten members were revealed sequentially through teaser content and the daily reality series SIGNAL. The first sub-unit, Acid Angel from Asia, debuted with ACCESS on October 28, 2022.

2023 – Official Debut & Unit Activities

tripleS debuted as a full group with ASSEMBLE and “Rising” on February 13, 2023. The fandom name WAV was selected through fan voting. Throughout the year, new members were revealed up to S20 and multiple units debuted, including LOVElution and EVOLution. The ballad unit Aria was formed, and the project unit NXT introduced the next members. The group also won the MAMA Award for Best New Female Artist.

2024 – Complete 24 Members & Global Expansion

The final four members were revealed in April, completing the 24-member lineup. On May 8, tripleS released ASSEMBLE24 as a full group. They achieved their first music show win with all members present. The official dance unit Visionary Vision was formed, and the group began Japanese promotions, including the unit ∞!.

2025 – Tours, ASSEMBLE25 & tripleS Universe

tripleS launched their second world tour tripleS Come True. The full group returned with ASSEMBLE25, with “Are You Alive?” selected through fan voting. They continued global concerts and unit activities, and fans formed the new “tripleS Universe” units: Moon, Sun, Neptune and Zenith.

On November 24, tripleS msnz released their first mini album Beyond Beauty. It includes the full-group winter song “Christmas Alone” and one title track for each unit: “Cameo Love” (Moon), “Bubble Gum Girl” (Sun), “Fly Up” (Neptune), and “Q&A” (Zenith).
